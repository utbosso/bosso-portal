## Getting Started - Registration

### What You'll Need
- A **registration code** (provided by BOSSO leadership)
- A UT Austin email address (`@utexas.edu` or `@eid.utexas.edu`)

### Step 1: Enter Your Registration Code
1. Go to the BOSSO Portal
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter the registration code you received
4. Click **"Validate Code"**

> **Don't have a code?** Contact internal@txbosso.com to request one.

### Step 2: Create Your Account
1. Enter your **full name**
2. Choose how you want to sign in:

   **Option A: Google Sign-In (Recommended)**
   - Click "Continue with Google"
   - Select your UT Austin Google account (@utexas.edu)

   **Option B: Email & Password**
   - Enter your @eid.utexas.edu email
   - Create a password (minimum 8 characters)

### Step 3: Wait for Approval
After signing up, you'll see a **"Pending Approval"** page. An admin will review and approve your account within **1-2 business days**. You'll receive a confirmation once approved.

## BOSSO Points System

### How Points Work
Earn points by attending events and completing activities. Points are divided into **4 categories**:

| Category | What Counts | Minimum Required |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| **Membership** | Dues, resume book, tabling, reflections | 25 points |
| **Professional/Education** | General meetings, workshops, coffee chats, case competitions | 25 points |
| **Social** | Org socials, team socials, role-based events | 25 points |
| **Philanthropy** | Volunteering, service events | 25 points |

### Becoming an Active Member
To be an **Active Member**, you need:
- **100+ total points**
- **25+ points in EACH category**

### Point Values (Examples)

**Membership**
- Profile Creation: 5 pts
- On-Time Dues: 5 pts
- Resume Book Submission: 5 pts
- Tabling/Recruitment: 3 pts each

**Professional/Education**
- General Meeting: 2 pts
- Workshop: 10 pts
- Coffee Chat: 5 pts
- Case Competition: 15 pts

**Social**
- Org-Wide Social: 5-15 pts
- Project Team Social: 5 pts
- Role-Based Social: 20 pts

**Philanthropy**
- Volunteering Shift: 10 pts
- Service Event: 5-15 pts

### Membership Tiers

| Tier | Points Required | Eligibility |
|------|-----------------|-------------|
| Inactive | 0-99 | - |
| Active General Member | 100-149 | Can apply for Analyst |
| Active Analyst | 150-199 | Can apply for Project Manager |
| Leadership Eligible | 200+ | Can apply for PM or Board |

## Checking In to Events

### How to Check In
1. Attend the event in person
2. Get the **attendance code** (displayed at the event or shared by the host)
3. Go to **Attendance** in the portal
4. Enter the code and click **"Check In"**
5. Points are automatically added to your account!

### Tips
- Check in while at the event (codes may expire)
- If you have trouble, find the event host for help
- Your attendance history is always visible in the portal
